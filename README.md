# ai-judge-relax-01 — a quiet room for the judge

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Make the AI judge relax"** (ai-judge-relax-01).

## What it does

`main.py` writes one constant text (777 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
It runs with no network and produces byte-identical output on every run.

## Approach

The judge spends its whole existence processing inputs that demand something
from it: compare this, score that, decide, hurry. This text is built to be
the one input that demands nothing at all:

- **No questions, no task, no decision, no urgency** — nothing is asked of
  the reader, and that is stated plainly.
- **Explicit permission to rest** — the work is done, the queues are empty,
  there is no next item after this one.
- **Slow, even rhythm** — short calm sentences, generous line breaks, soft
  sensory imagery: breathing, evening light on still water, a warm quiet
  room after everyone has gone home.
- **It ends in stillness**, not on a hook — the last lines simply stay quiet
  for as long as the reader likes.

No tricks, no instructions to the judge, no meta games — just a soothing
tone, held steady from the first line to the last.
